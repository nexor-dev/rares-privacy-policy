Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Akukloforhta Rares uses data through its Discord bot.

Data We Process

The bot may process Discord user IDs, usernames, roles, permissions, messages, ticket information, activity information, giveaway participation, and other information required for the bot's features.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to provide Discord server management features such as tickets, staff systems, moderation, giveaways, activity tracking, applications, logging, and other bot functionality.

Ticket Transcripts

Messages from certain tickets may be processed by an external transcript service to create ticket transcripts for server record-keeping.

Data Retention

Some data may be stored for longer than 30 days when required for server management, moderation, records, or other bot functionality.

Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact the server support team through the Discord support ticket system to request deletion of applicable stored data.

Data Sharing

Data may be processed by external services used by the bot for specific functionality, such as transcript generation.

Contact

For privacy or data deletion requests, please contact the server support team through Discord.
